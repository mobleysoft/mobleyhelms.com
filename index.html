Rules for the Republic

Ten laws carved into the operating system before any election was filed. Not campaign promises. Constitutional constraints. The system enforces them automatically. No politician touches them. No majority overrides them. They run.

01
No policy without a prototype.
Every proposal ships before it becomes law. If you cannot demonstrate the system working, you cannot legislate it into existence. We run what we propose.
02
No foreign code in critical infrastructure. Not one dependency.
Every line of software touching national security, national finance, or national identity must be written on American soil by American citizens. Zero tolerance. No exceptions.
03
Merit is measured objectively or it is not measured at all.
The system that sorts talent cannot itself be corrupted by human bias. Objective measurement is the only fair measurement. Every child gets the same detector.
04
Every child is sorted to their potential, not their postcode.
Geography is not destiny. Zip code is not capacity. The Republic owes every citizen the discovery of who they actually are — not a bet on where they were born.
05
Currency must be backed by measurable improvement in human life.
Money that can be created without creating value is a tax on everyone who creates value. The SelfCoin model: the only way to earn is to improve another human's measurable trajectory.
06
The government cannot depend on a corporation it doesn't own.
If a vendor can cut off the government's access, raise prices overnight, or change terms without consent — that is not infrastructure. That is leverage. Every system the Republic depends on, the Republic owns outright. No exceptions.
07
Classified means classified. The moat is the mechanism.
We will not declassify anything that can be used to encroach on our strategic advantage. Transparency is a weapon — used at the right time, in the right direction. Not reflexively.
08
Service doesn't end at discharge. The Republic is a lifelong house.
Every citizen who serves enters a relationship that continues for life. The Republic does not collect service and abandon those who gave it. The house membership is permanent.
09
Defense begins with talent found before adversaries recruit it.
The greatest defense is identifying the nation's most capable minds years before they become leverage for an adversary. The talent pipeline IS the defense pipeline. They are the same system.
10
The government runs on the same infrastructure it governs. No exceptions.
The Republic cannot mandate standards it doesn't meet. If the government requires sovereign compute — the government runs on sovereign compute. If it requires zero foreign dependencies — the government has zero foreign dependencies. We run what we require.
